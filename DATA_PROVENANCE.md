# Data Provenance

**Source:** MPLADS public dashboard at `mplads.mospi.gov.in`

## Datasets Provided for SIH26102
1. **`Allocated Limit for Honble MPs.xlsx`**
   - **Scope:** 543 MPs
   - **Content:** Sanctioned limits and cumulative expenditure across MPs and states.

2. **`Expenditure on Completed and On-going Works as on Date.xlsx`**
   - **Scope:** ~12,000 transaction records
   - **Content:** Payment ledger containing individual disbursements to vendors, mapped to specific works.

**Extraction Date:** September 2026
**Licence:** No explicit licence terms attached; data is published on a public government dashboard.

## Known Data Limitations in This Extract
- **Physical Progress:** No percentage or status of physical progress is included in the provided dataset.
- **Dates:** No sanction date, expected completion date, or completion status is available. Only payment dates are present.
- **Geospatial:** No GPS coordinates or block-level locations are included.
