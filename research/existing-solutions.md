# Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis

## 1. Official MPLADS Dashboard
- **What it does**: Provides overall statistics, fund utilization rates, and project counts.
- **Missing**: Multi-dimensional risk scoring, peer benchmarking, and predictive insights. Relies on humans to spot specific anomalies.

## 2. PAIMANA & General Government Monitoring Systems
- **What they do**: Track physical and financial progress, often with geo-tagging.
- **Missing**: They are primarily descriptive reporting tools rather than proactive risk-intelligence engines. They lack automated similarity detection for potential duplicate works.

## 3. Our Differentiation
"Existing systems provide monitoring, reporting, and descriptive analytics. Our platform adds an explainable AI-driven risk intelligence layer that compares projects with relevant peers, detects multi-dimensional anomalies, predicts emerging risks, and prioritizes projects for human investigation."
