# Problem Understanding: SIH26102

## 1. What is MPLADS?
The Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) enables MPs to recommend developmental work in their constituencies, focusing on creating durable community assets (e.g., drinking water, education, health infrastructure).

## 2. Project Lifecycle
1. **MP Recommendation**: MP identifies a need and recommends a project.
2. **Approval/Sanction**: District Authority validates and sanctions the work.
3. **Fund Release**: Funds are allocated/released to the Implementing Agency.
4. **Execution**: The agency starts physical work.
5. **Expenditure & Physical Progress**: Funds are utilized; milestones are reported.
6. **Completion**: Asset is created and handed over.
7. **Monitoring**: Continuous tracking of financial and physical progress.

## 3. Why is Monitoring Difficult?
Monitoring thousands of projects across various districts involves massive amounts of administrative, financial, and geographic data. Human investigators cannot continuously cross-reference every project's cost, timeline, and location with peer projects to spot anomalies. 

## 4. Identifying the Real Problem
The challenge is not simply storing data; it's prioritizing **which projects require human attention first**. 
The system must identify unusual patterns (anomalies, deviations, potential non-compliance) and present them with clear evidence so authorized officials can investigate further. 
**Core Principle:** AI prioritizes investigation; humans make the final decision.
